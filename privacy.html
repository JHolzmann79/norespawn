No Respawn Clipper: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

No Respawn Clipper is a personal tool used only by the owner of the No Respawn channels to edit gaming clips and

post them to the owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other

people log in to it.

What data the app uses

  • The owner's own account access tokens for YouTube and Instagram, used only to upload the owner's clips and

    • to read view counts for those clips. Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.

  • Basic account details (account name and ID) to show which account is connected.
  • Performance numbers (views, likes, comments, shares) for videos the app posted, to learn which clips do best.

    • What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about other people, followers or commenters.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use data for advertising.

    • Where data is stored

    All data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app.

    Deleting data

    To delete all data, the owner disconnects the account in the app's Settings page (which deletes the stored

    token) and removes the app's data folder. Access can also be revoked at any time in Instagram

    (Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites) or in the Google account's security settings.

    Questions or deletion requests: contact the No Respawn channel owner through the channel's profile.
