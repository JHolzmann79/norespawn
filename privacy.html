No Respawn Clipper: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

No Respawn Clipper is a personal tool used only by the owner of the No Respawn channels. It cuts short vertical clips from public gaming live streams (crediting the streamer whose stream each clip comes from) and, after the owner reviews and approves each clip, posts it to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other people sign in to it.

What data the app uses

  • The owner's own account access tokens for YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, used only to upload the owner's approved clips and to read statistics for those clips. Tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer.
  • Basic account details (account name and ID) to show which account is connected.
  • Statistics for videos the app posted (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time, follower counts), to learn which clips perform best.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about other people, viewers, followers or commenters.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use any data for advertising.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's approved clips to the owner's YouTube channel and to read the statistics of those videos. Use of the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

TikTok and Instagram

The app uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, and Instagram's Graph API, only to post the owner's approved clips to the owner's own accounts and read their statistics. TikTok handles data as described in the TikTok Privacy Policy; Instagram as described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.

Deleting data

To delete all data, the owner disconnects each account in the app's Settings page (which deletes the stored token) and removes the app's data folder. Access can also be revoked at any time in the Google security settings above, in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions), or in Instagram (Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites).

Questions or deletion requests: contact the No Respawn channel owner through the channel's profile.
